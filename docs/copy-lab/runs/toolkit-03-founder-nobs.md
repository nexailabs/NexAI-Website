# Toolkit heroes — Candidate 03 (Founder / NO-BS) — REDO

**Angle:** Lead every hero with the proof, not the author — these tools, prompts, and notes earned
their place by running on real work before we published them, free and ungated. No plumbing, no
credentials, no contortions: where the original line was already true, I tightened it.

---

## App Vault

- **Eyebrow:** `App Vault · {N} tools`
- **Headline (H1):** `The stack we *actually* run on.`
- **Sub:** `Every tool we open daily — what it is, why we picked it, what it replaced. We pay for these; they aren't affiliate links.`

*why (sub):* Headline was already good — left it. Tightened the sub to claim → proof: "what it
replaced" is the proof a real team made a real switch, and "we pay for these" makes the
no-affiliate point land instead of leaning on "written by us" (a credential).

---

## Prompt Hub

- **Eyebrow:** `Prompt Hub`
- **Headline (H1):** `*[Prompts | SOPs | skills.md]* that *survived* real client work.`
  - **Rotating first word:** `Prompts` → `SOPs` → `skills.md`
- **Sub:** `Single-shot prompts, the runbooks our team follows, and skills your agent installs. Some free, some paid.`

*why (headline):* The original "survived a month of real work" was already a clean, true line — so I
sharpened, not contorted. Dropped only the "month" (reads as a clock); "survived real client work"
keeps the full battle-tested wedge — this ran in production before we published it — in fewer words.
*why (sub):* Dropped "via Razorpay" — the payment vendor is noise the buyer doesn't care about;
"some free, some paid" carries the whole point. "Human runbooks" → "the runbooks our team follows"
and "packaged skills" → "skills your agent installs" say the same thing as a person would.

## Field Notes

- **Eyebrow:** `Field Notes`
- **Headline (H1):** `Essays, playbooks, and *build logs* from a company run on agents.`
- **Sub:** `Drawn from work we actually shipped — what we built, what broke, what we'd do again. No ghostwriters, no SEO filler, no recycled hot takes.`
- **Signature line:** `from the lab in Bengaluru.`

*why (sub):* THE FIX. The prior lead, "Written by the founders," is a credential — this reader's
answer is "so what?" Replaced it with the evidence: these notes come out of work we shipped, so
they carry receipts, not opinions. "What we built, what broke, what we'd do again" makes the proof
concrete and matches the homepage Toolkit promise ("the things we got wrong"). Kept the three honest
negatives — they're already sharp. Headline and Bengaluru signature untouched.

## What I'd A/B test next

1. **Field Notes lead:** "Drawn from work we actually shipped" vs. a harder first-person line —
   "We only write up things we ran ourselves" — does ownership beat the softer framing?
2. **Prompt Hub verb:** "*survived* real client work" vs. "*earned* on real client work" — does
   "survived" read as battle-tested grit, or as faint praise (barely made it)?
3. **App Vault proof:** "we pay for these" vs. a count of switches ("{N} tools, {M} of them
   replacements") — does a number beat the human aside?

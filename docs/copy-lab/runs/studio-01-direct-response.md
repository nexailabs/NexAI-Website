## 5. Process (how it works)

**Section title (desktop):**
`You upload. We ship. You do nothing in between.`
*why: resolves the duplicate-header flag — Showcase now sells the result, Process sells the hands-off service. This header makes the "done-for-you, not a tool you operate" point that separates Studio from the AI apps that burned them.*

**Section sub (desktop):**
`Send the product photos. Approve the looks. Get your assets. No casting, no location, no lighting, no reshoot day.`

**Steps — numbered `01` → `04`:**
*why: resolves the day-numbering flag. "Day 1–4" invents a fixed 4-day calendar we may not honor per order. Neutral `01–04` keeps the sequence without a fake SLA. The real turnaround claim (3-5 days) stays in the meta and the mobile sub, where it's a service promise we can stand behind.*

1. **`Send Your Product Photos`** — `Mannequin, flat-lay, or ghost — whatever you already shot. No new photography on your end.`
2. **`We Do the Prep`** — `Our team cleans the background, stitches the garment, and builds the model map. You sit this part out.`
3. **`We Generate the Shoot`** — `Models, poses, scenes — matched to your brand. Any skin tone, any body type, any vibe you brief.`
4. **`We QA and Deliver`** — `A human reviews every frame, retouches if it's off, and ships it sized for Myntra, Amazon, or your Shopify.`
*why: rewrote step 4 to surface the human-QA + regenerate-if-off promise — this is the risk reversal a fabric-burned buyer needs inside the process, not just at the end.*

## What I'd A/B test next

1. Hero bridge: "On-model shots. No studio, no reshoots." vs. a cost-led "On-model shots, 80% cheaper than a studio." — does the money number or the logistics relief pull harder?
2. Final CTA headline: the risk-reversal "before you pay a rupee" vs. the original outcome framing "Launch the collection, skip the shoot."
3. Showcase sub: fabric-promise "Same print. Same stitch." vs. a speed-promise "Marketplace-ready in 3-5 days." — which objection (quality vs. turnaround) converts this ICP?
4. Process header: "You do nothing in between" (hands-off) vs. a control framing "You approve every look before it ships."
5. Gallery CTA: "See the fabric up close" vs. a volume flex "Browse 500+ before you book" — proof-of-quality vs. proof-of-scale.

# Run 04 — Minimalist

**Angle:** Subtract until the product is the only thing left. The hero is a 3-word claim plus one sentence; the Sales card stops narrating and just shows the work being done.
**Canonical 6-agent roster:** `Sales · Social · Creatives · Operations · Research · Finance` (matches the orbit's strongest demos; drops the abstract "CEO" rotating word from the hero cycle so every word names a function a founder can picture).

---

## 1. Hero section

- **Eyebrow:** `Agents that do the work`
  - *Cut "Your business on autopilot." Autopilot is a metaphor; "do the work" is the actual thing.*
- **Headline static prefix:** `Agents that run`
- **Rotating words:** `Sales`, `Social`, `Creatives`, `Operations`, `Research`, `Finance`
- **Headline trailing char:** `.`
  - *Reads: "Agents that run Sales." Three words, one verb, one named function. The verb carries it.*
- **Subtext / lede:** `We build it, run it in your stack, and hand you the reviews. You sign off; it gets smarter.`
  - *Killed the four empty verbs (understand, design, develop, train). Replaced with the only two things a burned buyer cares about: it ships into their stack, and they stay in control.*
- **Primary CTA:** `Spec my first agent` → opens Cal.com booking widget
- **Ghost CTA:** `See one work` → anchors to `#anatomy` (the Anatomy section, which exists; retargeted off the stale `#how`)

**What I cut:** The metaphor eyebrow and the four-verb sentence — both described instead of stating. One claim, one sentence, one door. A problem-aware founder still gets: what (agents that run a function), how it's safe (you sign off), and why care (it compounds).

---

## 2. Sales agent card

- **Card title:** `Sales`
- **Role:** `Pipeline`
  - *Cut "& Outreach." The pipeline is the outcome; outreach is one step inside it.*
- **Description (off-screen, aria/SEO):** `Finds leads, drafts outreach, qualifies inbound, books the meeting.`
  - *Cut "closes the room" — clever but vague. "Books the meeting" is what the demo actually shows.*
- **Status badge:** `BOOKING MEETING`
- **Chart label:** `Pipeline · $2.4M` · **trend:** `↑ 34% WoW`
  - *Kept as demo content per constraint §4.*
- **Workflow steps:**
  1. `Lead in CRM` — `Aria Sharma · Head of Growth, The Whole Truth` — DONE
  2. `Profile enriched` — `Series B · 80 people · Mumbai` — DONE
  3. `Discovery call` — `"We need AI photoshoots for our protein launch…"` — DONE · `3m42s`
  4. `Invite drafted` — `Wed 12:30pm · 30 min · needs your confirm` — CURRENT (needs you)
  - *Trimmed each step title to noun + verb. "New lead detected in CRM" → "Lead in CRM." The state (DONE) already says it's done; the word "detected" is filler.*
- **Action buttons:** `Confirm` (primary) / `Reschedule` (ghost)
  - *Cut "meeting" — the card is already about a meeting.*
- **Reschedule path — "Checking calendar" text:** `3 open slots`
- **Reschedule slot pills:**
  - `Thu 10:00am` — `30 min · same agenda`
  - `Thu 3:00pm` — `45 min · w/ co-founder`
  - `Fri 11:00am` — `30 min · post demo`
- **Confirm-button morphed label after click:** `Booked`
- **Post-confirm cascade:**
  - `Confirmation sent` — `aria@thewholetruthfoods.com`
  - `Saved to CRM` — `Pipeline +$24K`
  - *Cut "your" from "Saved to your CRM" — it's already their CRM.*
- **Cycle summary:** `Booked end-to-end`
  - *Cut "28m" — it's a time claim, and the point is that it ran itself, not that it ran fast.*
- **Post-cycle CTA:** `Want this running your pipeline?` / `Book a call →`
  - *Cut "15-min" (time commitment) and "with NexAI" (they know whose site they're on).*

**What I cut:** Every word the surrounding UI already says — "meeting," "your," the elapsed-time summary, the duration on the CTA. The demo facts stayed; the narration around them went.

## What I'd A/B test next

1. Hero verb: `Agents that run Sales.` vs. `Agents that close Sales.` — does a sharper verb lift or overclaim?
2. Hero CTA: `Spec my first agent` vs. `See it for my business` — intent-led vs. curiosity-led.
3. Sales cycle summary: `Booked end-to-end` vs. no summary line at all — does the closing line add or clutter?
4. Roster word swap: `Finance` vs. keeping `CEO` in the rotation — which reads as more credible to a Seed–Series B operator?

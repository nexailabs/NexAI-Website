# Studio run — Candidate 03 (Founder / NO-BS)

**Angle:** Lead with the scar. This buyer got burned by an AI tool that melted their prints, so
every line earns trust by naming the failure mode first, then showing the mechanism that prevents it
— samples before you pay, humans on QA, you review fabric before delivery. Speed and price are the
proof, not the pitch.

## 5. Process (how it works)

**Desktop:**

- **Section title:** `How a shoot runs.`
  - *Why: resolves the flagged duplicate. Showcase = the result; Process = the mechanism. This header is a plain label for "here's exactly what happens," which is what builds trust with a burned buyer.*
- **Section sub:** `You send what you've got. We prep, generate, and QA every shot. You approve before anything ships. 3–5 days, start to delivery.`
  - *Why: the original sub listed what you skip (casting, location, lighting). Better to state the real mechanism and the SLA the business actually offers (studio-brief §4 permits "3–5 days"). The approval gate is the trust spine — say it in the sub, not buried in step 4.*

- **Steps** (renumbered `01` → `04`, decorative "Day 1–4" removed):
  - *Why: studio-brief §4 flags "Day 1 / Day 2 / Day 3 / Day 4" as an invented fixed calendar. The real, defensible claim is the 3–5 day total turnaround — not a per-step daily clock. Neutral "01–04" keeps the sequence, drops the fake timeline.*

  1. **`01 — Send your product photos`** — `Mannequin, flat-lay, or ghost. Whatever you already shoot for the catalogue. No new photography on your end.`
  2. **`02 — We prep the garment`** — `Our team cleans the background, reconstructs the garment, and maps it to a model. You do nothing here.`
  3. **`03 — We generate the shoot`** — `Models, poses, scenes — matched to your brand. Any skin tone, body type, or vibe you brief. The print and weave are held to the original.`
     - *Why: added the one clause this buyer needs in the generation step — that fabric fidelity is enforced here, where their last tool failed them.*
  4. **`04 — A human QAs it, then you approve`** — `Our team reviews every frame and retouches if needed. If a fabric reads wrong, we regenerate — before it reaches you. Then it ships ready for Myntra, Amazon, or your Shopify.`
     - *Why: surfaced the regenerate-if-off promise from the brief (§3 trust spine). "Before it reaches you" tells the buyer the failure case is caught on our side, not theirs.*

## What I'd A/B test next

1. **Final-CTA headline:** "See your own fabric, before you decide." vs. the original outcome framing "Launch the collection. Skip the shoot." — trust-led vs. outcome-led for a burned buyer.
2. **Meta description:** fabric-first ("the fabric stays the fabric") vs. price-first ("80% cheaper") — which wins the marketplace-manager click.
3. **Bridge headline:** "No casting, no studio, no reshoot." vs. a price-forward "A fraction of a studio shoot." — does cost or logistics pain pull harder.
4. **Showcase sub:** the dare ("check the fabric yourself") vs. a neutral "see before/after by category" — does inviting scrutiny raise or lower bounce.
5. **Process numbering:** neutral "01–04" vs. the real "3–5 days" stamped on the section header — does an explicit SLA badge lift booking intent.

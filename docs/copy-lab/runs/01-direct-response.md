# Run 01 — Direct-Response (Hero + Sales card only)

**Angle:** AIDA hero, PAS micro-arc on the Sales card. Lead with the outcome the founder already feels missing — a booked meeting — not the feature. Reframe the demo as *the agent doing your job while you watch*, with a human-sign-off beat as risk reversal.
**Canonical 6 hero rotating words:** `Sales · Social · Creatives · Operations · CEO · Research` — kept because these are the six the orbit actually demos and the rotating word is driven by the live active card; one roster, used in the hero and the orbit it controls.

---

## 1. Hero section

- **Eyebrow** (teal uppercase): `Hire your first agent, not your fifth employee`
  - *Why: trades the vague "autopilot" promise for the concrete trade the reader is weighing — headcount vs. an agent that does the work.*
- **Headline static prefix:** `AI Agents for`
- **Rotating words** (driven by the orbit's active agent): `Sales`, `Social`, `Creatives`, `Operations`, `CEO`, `Research`
- **Headline trailing char:** `.`
- **Subtext / lede:** `One agent that runs the function — finds the work, does it, and waits for your one-click sign-off before anything ships.`
  - *Why: kills the four empty verbs (understand/design/develop/train). States what it does and the human-in-the-loop trust beat in one line, benefit-first.*
- **Primary CTA:** `Show me an agent for my business` → opens Cal.com booking widget
  - *Why: benefit-loaded, reader-framed invitation over the generic "Build My Agent." Same link, lower perceived risk.*
- **Ghost CTA:** `Watch one run, live below` → anchors to the Anatomy section (`#anatomy`)
  - *Why: retargets the stale `#how` anchor to a section that exists, and promises a proof, not a tour.*

## 2. Sales agent card

PAS arc: **Problem** — leads go cold while you draft. **Agitate** — the meeting you never booked. **Solution** — the agent already booked it; you just sign off.

- **Card title:** `Sales`
- **Role:** `Books the meeting you'd have missed`
  - *Why: role line reframed from the feature label ("Pipeline & Outreach") to the outcome the founder feels.*
- **Description (off-screen, aria/SEO):** `Finds the lead, writes the cold open, qualifies the inbound, and books the call — you confirm.`
  - *Why: ends on the human-sign-off beat; keeps the same four jobs as concrete verbs.*
- **Status badge:** `BOOKING MEETING`
- **Chart label:** `Pipeline · $2.4M` · **trend:** `↑ 34% WoW`
  - *(Demo content kept as-is — illustrative, not a company claim.)*
- **Workflow steps** (timeline above the action buttons):
  1. `New lead detected in CRM` — `Aria Sharma · Head of Growth, The Whole Truth` — DONE
  2. `Enriched Aria's profile` — `Series B · 80 employees · Mumbai` — DONE
  3. `Discovery call` — `"We need AI photoshoots for our protein bar launch…"` — DONE · elapsed `3m42s`
  4. `Meeting drafted — your call to send` — `Wed 12:30pm · 30 min · awaiting your confirm` — CURRENT (needs you)
  - *Why: step 4 title states the only thing left is the reader's one click — the sign-off beat that earns trust.*
- **Action buttons:** `Confirm meeting` (primary) / `Reschedule` (ghost)
- **Reschedule path — "Checking calendar" text:** `3 open slots found`
- **Reschedule slot pills:**
  - `Thu 10:00am` — `30 min · same agenda`
  - `Thu 3:00pm` — `45 min · w/ co-founder`
  - `Fri 11:00am` — `30 min · post product demo`
- **Confirm-button morphed label after click:** `Booked`
- **Post-confirm cascade** (fans in after Confirm):
  - `Confirmation sent` — `aria@thewholetruthfoods.com`
  - `Saved to your CRM` — `Pipeline +$24K`
- **Cycle summary:** `Found, qualified, booked — all you did was say yes.`
  - *Why: swaps the time-stamp ("28m end-to-end" — a clock, and banned) for the value: the work happened without you doing it. Honors the no-time-commitment constraint.*
- **Post-cycle CTA:** `Want this booking meetings for your business?` / `Book a 15-min call with NexAI →`
  - *Why: benefit-first question; the CTA link and label are unchanged (real Cal.com path).*

## What I'd A/B test next
1. Eyebrow: `Hire your first agent, not your fifth employee` vs. `Stop drafting outreach. Start confirming meetings.` — headcount-cost angle vs. midnight-ops-relief angle.
2. Primary CTA: `Show me an agent for my business` vs. `Spec my first agent` — soft demo-ask vs. concrete build-ask, for which pulls more qualified bookings.
3. Cycle summary: outcome line (`all you did was say yes`) vs. a number-led line that isn't a clock, e.g. `1 click from you, 1 meeting on the calendar`.
4. Ghost CTA: scroll-to-proof (`Watch one run, live below`) vs. clicking the live Sales card itself as the proof — does sending them to Anatomy or keeping them in the orbit convert better?

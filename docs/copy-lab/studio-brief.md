# Studio brief — read before rewriting the /studio page

Voice rules and banned words come from [`brief.md`](brief.md) §4–5 (same house voice). This file
**overrides** the homepage brief on **audience, offer, and the time-claim rule** — the Studio page
sells a different thing to a different buyer. The original copy you are rewriting is in
[`studio-original.md`](studio-original.md) (FAQ excluded — do not rewrite FAQ).

---

## 1. What NexAI Studio sells (different from the homepage)

A **done-for-you AI photoshoot service** for D2C brands — **apparel, jewelry, and beauty/cosmetics
alike** (the live showcase has Kurta Sets, Western Wear, Saree, Menswear, **Jewelry**, and
**Cosmetics**). You send flat product photos (mannequin, flat-lay, ghost, packshot) → NexAI returns
marketplace-ready shots — on-model where it fits, styled product/lifestyle where it doesn't — with
models, poses, and scenes matched to the brand. A real studio that happens to use AI, **not a tool
the customer operates.** The human team preps, runs, QAs, and ships.

> **Scope warning:** do NOT narrow the whole page to garments/fabric. A "fabric fidelity" frame
> alone silently drops the jewelry and cosmetics buyers. The promise must be **product-agnostic.**

## 2. Who reads /studio (the Studio ICP — NOT the homepage ICP)

- **Indian D2C brands across apparel, jewelry, and beauty/cosmetics** — founders, ecommerce/catalogue
  managers, brand leads. Real clients span garments (Yufta, Leemboodi), **jewelry (DBJ)**, and
  **beauty/cosmetics (Thrive, Soil & Earth)** — write to all three, not just clothing.
- They list on **Myntra, Amazon, Flipkart, Shopify** and need a constant stream of on-model
  product images and video.
- **Their pain with the status quo:** physical shoots are expensive, slow, and logistically heavy —
  casting, models, location, lighting, studio rental, reshoots. Catalogue volume makes it worse.
- **Their scar:** they've tried AI image tools that **butchered the product** — a melted print, a
  warped weave, a mangled gemstone, plastic-looking skin, a logo turned to mush. Skepticism about
  quality is the #1 objection, and it applies to garments, jewelry, and cosmetics alike. (The FAQ
  already answers this — match that honesty.)
- This buyer thinks in **cost per shot, turnaround, and marketplace compliance**, not abstractions.

## 3. What makes Studio different (the levers to pull)

- **Cost** — a fraction of a physical shoot. (Site currently claims "80% cheaper than studios.")
- **Done-for-you service**, not a prompt tool. You do nothing; the team handles brief → ship.
- **Product fidelity** — the garment's weave, the gemstone's facets, the bottle's label, the skin's
  texture all render true. Built for real products, not generic images. Samples before delivery;
  regenerate if it's off. This is the trust spine — lean on it, but keep it **product-agnostic**
  (the failure this answers is "will it wreck *my product*", whatever the product is — not just fabric).
- **Marketplace-ready** — correct ratios, clean backgrounds, model-on-white or lifestyle.
- **You own the images** — full commercial rights, use anywhere, forever.
- **Range** — any skin tone, body type, pose, vibe, to brief. Stills *and* ad-ready video.
- **Proof** — "50+ brands", "60,000+ shipped", "500+ creatives" gallery, named brand logos
  (DBJ, Leemboodi, Yufta, STF, XYXX, etc.). These are real — keep/strengthen them.

## 4. The time-claim rule — RELAXED for Studio (read carefully)

On the homepage, time commitments are banned. On Studio, **turnaround speed is a genuine,
factual differentiator** (AI shoots are faster than physical studios), so:
- **Allowed:** concrete service turnaround the business actually offers — e.g. "3–5 days",
  "days, not weeks". Treat these as a service SLA, not a vague marketing promise.
- **Reconsider:** the decorative **"Day 1 / Day 2 / Day 3 / Day 4"** step numbering in the process
  section is arbitrary (it implies a fixed 4-day calendar that may not be real). If you keep a
  numbered process, prefer neutral step numbers ("01–04") **or** flag it — don't invent a timeline.
- **Still banned:** made-up deadlines, fake urgency ("only 3 slots left this week"), and any time
  claim you can't stand behind.
- If unsure whether a specific time reference is real, **flag it** rather than inventing or deleting.

## 5. Other hard constraints (unchanged)

- **No fluff adjectives** — banned list in `brief.md` §5.2.
- **No invented claims** — don't inflate "50+ brands" or "60,000+ shipped" beyond what's on the
  current site. Keep the real numbers; don't manufacture new ones.
- **Don't break real links/CTAs** — `Book a Free Call` → Cal.com; `Open Gallery` → /studio/gallery.
- **Keep it honest about quality** — this ICP has been burned; match the FAQ's "we don't butcher
  fabric, you review samples first" candor instead of over-promising.
